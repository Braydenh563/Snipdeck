# Audit: Settings, Help and Toast window bindings (agent `bindings`)

Read-only, by reading code (no cargo run, no source edits, no git). Short paths: `app/` = `crates/snipdeck-app/src/`,
`ui/` = `crates/snipdeck-ui/ui/`. Callback wiring was checked by diffing every `callback` declared on
`SettingsPage`, `HelpWindow` and `ToastWindow` against the `on_*` calls in the bindings: every exported callback is
wired exactly once (the two `close_requested` wires in Settings are the page callback and the window's title-bar X,
by design; `added`/`removed`/`chosen`/`reveal` belong to the inner `ProgramList`/`PasteRuleList` components and are
handled in Slint). Slider and stepper values are clamped by `Settings::sanitize` (app/settings.rs:1176-1189) after
`update_settings`, so the `u32::try_from(..).unwrap_or(0)` conversions are safe.

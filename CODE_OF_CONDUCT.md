# Code of Conduct

Snipdeck follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text, which applies in its entirety, is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>. This page is a summary.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps: being kind and patient, respecting other viewpoints and experiences, giving and
accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and caring about what is best for the
community.

Behaviour that is not acceptable: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insults or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without
permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place where someone
represents the project in public).

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@Braydenh563](https://github.com/Braydenh563)) is responsible for clarifying and enforcing
these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not
follow them, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they judge inappropriate,
threatening, offensive or harmful. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private channel: open a private security advisory at
<https://github.com/Braydenh563/Snipdeck/security/advisories/new> and title it "Conduct report", or
message the maintainer privately on GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence. A dedicated contact address
will be added here when there is one.
